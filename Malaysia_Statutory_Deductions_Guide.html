EPF — Employees Provident Fund (KWSP)

Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja  ·  Retirement savings scheme

EPF is a mandatory retirement savings contribution deducted from every employee's monthly salary. Both the employee and employer make contributions into the employee's EPF account. Managed by KWSP (Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja).
Employee Category Employee Rate Employer Rate Notes
Age below 60 (Standard) 11% 13% / 12% Employer: 13% if salary ≤ RM5,000  |  12% if salary > RM5,000
Age below 60 (Opted lower) 9% 13% / 12% Employee can opt for 9% voluntarily
Age 60–75 5.5% 6.5% Reduced rates for near-retirement age
Age above 75 5.5% 6% Lowest rates for senior employees
Our System (Current) 2% — Simplified rate used by many companies
⚠️ Our System Note: The system currently calculates EPF at 2% of basic salary (employee share only). This is a simplified rate used by many companies as a conservative baseline. The standard statutory rate is 11%. This can be adjusted in system settings.
// EPF Calculation (current system)
Monthly EPF = Basic Salary × 2%

// Example: RM 5,000 salary
Monthly EPF = 5,000 × 0.02 = RM 100.00

SOCSO — Social Security Organisation (PERKESO)

Pertubuhan Keselamatan Sosial  ·  Employment injury & invalidity protection

SOCSO provides employment injury insurance and invalidity pension for employees. Contributions are based on a fixed amount lookup table (not a percentage), with the salary ceiling at RM 5,000. Employees earning above RM 5,000 pay the same fixed ceiling amount.

PCB / MTD — Monthly Tax Deduction

Potongan Cukai Bulanan  ·  Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri (LHDN)  ·  Income tax

PCB (Potongan Cukai Bulanan) is monthly income tax withheld by the employer on behalf of LHDN. It uses progressive tax brackets applied to the annual chargeable income, then divided by 12 to get the monthly amount. Chargeable income = Annual gross − EPF annual − Personal relief (RM 9,000).

Step 1 — Calculate Annual Chargeable Income

Annual Gross = Monthly Salary × 12
Annual EPF Deduction = Monthly EPF × 12
Personal Relief = RM 9,000 (standard individual relief)
Chargeable Income = Annual Gross − Annual EPF − RM 9,000

Step 2 — Apply Progressive Tax Brackets

Chargeable Income (Annual) Tax Rate Tax on Band Cumulative Tax
First RM 5,000 0% RM 0 RM 0
RM 5,001 – RM 20,000 1% RM 150 RM 150
RM 20,001 – RM 35,000 3% RM 450 RM 600
RM 35,001 – RM 50,000 8% RM 1,200 RM 1,800
RM 50,001 – RM 70,000 13% RM 2,600 RM 4,400

Step 3 — Monthly PCB

Monthly PCB = Annual Tax ÷ 12
